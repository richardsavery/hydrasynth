How is the rhythm made?

A musical clock repeats 16 steps. Hits / 16 spreads hits across them; 4 hits gives a hit every fourth step. Tempo and Steps / beat set the speed. Rotate moves the hits, Repeats subdivides them, Swing alternates long and short steps, and Accent changes their emphasis. Gate controls note length and Note chance adds rests.

Your signals change those settings as the recording advances. The rhythm is generated from the data, rather than detected from recorded beats. At 120 BPM and 4 steps per beat, the pattern lasts two seconds.
